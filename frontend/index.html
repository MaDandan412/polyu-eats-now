PolyU Eats Now

Good food, less guesswork.

A campus food-ordering tool for students and staff at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (Hong Kong PolyU). See which restaurants currently accept online orders, without checking each restaurant link individually.

为香港理工大学师生整合校园点餐入口，查看哪些餐厅现在可以在线下单。少点几个链接，少跑一次空路。

為香港理工大學師生整合校園點餐入口，查看哪些餐廳現在可以在線下單。少點幾個連結，少跑一次空路。

Availability is checked against official ordering systems. This is an independent community project, not an official university application.
